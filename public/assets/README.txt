CUSTOM LOGO (optional)
======================
Put your logo picture here and name it exactly:
      public/assets/logo.png
   • Use a PNG with a TRANSPARENT background.
   • It replaces the "YOURBOOTH" wordmark EVERYWHERE automatically
     (the big one on the home page AND the small one at the top of each booth).

To resize the logo, open  src/components/Logo.jsx  and change the
height classes:
   • big (home)   ->  h-20 sm:h-24 md:h-28   (make the numbers bigger/smaller)
   • small (top)  ->  h-9

If logo.png is not here, the built-in text logo is shown instead.

(See also  assets/fish/README.txt  for custom fish,
 and  assets/polaroid/README.txt  for custom Polaroid frames.)
