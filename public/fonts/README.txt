Drop the "Child Hood" doodle font here as:  Childhood.ttf

Get it from 1001fonts (Child Hood by Rahagita Studio) or dafont, then rename to
Childhood.ttf. The app references /fonts/Childhood.ttf and will use it for the
countdown numbers and the Polaroid date stamp. Until then it falls back to the
"Gochi Hand" doodle font automatically.
